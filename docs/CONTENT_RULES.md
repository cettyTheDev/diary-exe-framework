# Content and evidence rules

1. Exact diary text is published only from an approved source page and retains its source ID and page number.
2. OCR is labeled `OCR / UNVERIFIED TRANSCRIPTION` until checked against the page image.
3. Editorial summaries are never styled or punctuated as quotations.
4. Third-party claims require an independent source record and remain labeled as claims.
5. `UNRESOLVED` is preferred to inference when date, speaker, page, or meaning is uncertain.
6. Relationships use typed verbs such as `mentioned`, `met`, `discussed`, `responded`, or `editorial link`; they never imply causality beyond their citations.
7. Every fixture sets `isFixture: true`, uses neutral placeholder language, and is visibly marked as synthetic in the interface.
8. The demo manifest is not a source document. Its page numbers and dates exercise UI paths only.
9. No person is automatically labeled deceptive, conspiratorial, or culpable.
10. Authoritative-source acquisition, copyright/public-record review, and checksum inventory are M2 gates.
